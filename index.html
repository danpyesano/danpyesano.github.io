PhD Researcher · Social Policy · Ulster University

Danny Pye

My PhD examines how illegal landfilling in Northern Ireland operates and how it can be disrupted. I use investigative ethnography, combining interviews with OSINT, records research, data analysis and GIS. It draws on my background as a journalist investigating environmental and state-corporate crime.
